## Fonts

`fonts/` holds the fonts Excalidraw publishes with its package, under their own licenses:
Excalifont, Virgil, Assistant, Cascadia Code, Liberation Sans, Lilita One and Nunito under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1, and Comic Shanns under the MIT License. The CJK font (Xiaolai) is left out
to keep the page small; CJK text renders in the system's font.
